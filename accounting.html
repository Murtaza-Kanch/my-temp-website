How We Work

Onsite or Online — You Choose

We adapt to your business. Whether you prefer our team at your office or fully remote cloud-based accounting, we deliver the same quality.

🏢 Onsite Accounting

Dedicated Accountant at Your Office

Our accountant visits your office on agreed days each week or month — working directly with your team, handling physical documents, and keeping your books up to date in person. Ideal for businesses that prefer face-to-face support and have a high volume of daily transactions.

  • Physical presence at your office
  • Handles cash, cheques, and paper documents
  • Works with your existing systems
  • Ideal for retail, F&B, and high-transaction businesses

Remote Cloud-Based Accounting

Your books are managed entirely online using cloud accounting software like Zoho Books, QuickBooks, or Xero. Documents are shared digitally, financial reports are delivered via email or a shared dashboard, and you get real-time visibility from anywhere in the world.

  • No office visits required
  • Real-time financial dashboard access
  • Secure cloud document sharing
  • Ideal for startups, remote teams, and lean businesses
