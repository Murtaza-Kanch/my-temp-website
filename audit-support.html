Audit Requirements

Who Needs an Audit in the UAE?

🏢

Mainland Companies

Most mainland LLCs and sole establishments are required to submit audited financials for their annual trade licence renewal.

🌐

Free Zone Companies

Most UAE Free Zones require an annual statutory audit as part of the licence renewal process. Timely submission is essential.

🏦

Bank Loan Applicants

UAE banks require at least 1–2 years of audited financial statements as part of any business loan or credit facility application.

📈

Investment-Ready Businesses

Businesses seeking investors or going through M&A transactions require credible, independently audited financial statements.
