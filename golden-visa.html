What Is It?

The UAE Golden Visa

The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residency programme that grants eligible individuals and their families the right to live, work, and study in the UAE for 5 or 10 years — renewable automatically.

Unlike standard UAE residency visas, the Golden Visa provides stability, flexibility, and a host of privileges — making it the preferred choice for investors, business owners, skilled professionals, and high-achieving individuals seeking long-term security in the UAE.

JSK & JYMMA manages the complete application process — from eligibility assessment and document preparation through to submission and final visa issuance — with full guidance at every step.

  • 5 or 10 year renewable residency
  • Sponsor spouse, children, and household staff
  • No mandatory exit from UAE required
  • 100% business ownership rights
  • Access to UAE banking and financial services

Eligibility Categories

Who Can Apply for UAE Golden Visa?

JSK & JYMMA specialises in three main Golden Visa pathways.

🏠

Property Investor Golden Visa

10 Year Visa

For investors who own UAE property worth a minimum of AED 2 million. The property must be fully owned (not mortgaged) or mortgaged at specific conditions.

  • Property value: minimum AED 2 million
  • Can include off-plan properties from approved developers
  • Include spouse and children
  • 10-year renewable residency

Government Fees: Approx. AED 2,800 – AED 4,500
(subject to applicable authority)

💼

Business Investor Golden Visa

5 or 10 Year Visa

For entrepreneurs and business owners with an active UAE business and a minimum investment threshold or annual tax contribution.

  • Active UAE trade licence required
  • Minimum paid-up capital or annual tax
  • Include immediate family members
  • 5 or 10 year residency depending on category

Government Fees: Approx. AED 3,000 – AED 5,500
(subject to applicable authority)

💰

Salary-Based Golden Visa

10 Year Visa

For UAE-based employees with a high monthly salary, working in the private sector with a valid UAE employment contract and Emirates ID.

  • Minimum salary: AED 30,000 per month
  • Valid UAE employment contract
  • Must be employed in UAE private sector
  • Include spouse and children

Government Fees: Approx. AED 2,800 – AED 4,000
(subject to applicable authority)
