The Founder's Story

From FMCG Sales Boy
to Tech Founder in UAE

At 17, Murtaza Kanchwala was selling consumer goods in the markets of India · carrying a bag full of products and a head full of ambition with nowhere to go yet. By 2015, he had made his first pivot into IT sales, working with companies that partnered with Google and Microsoft. He found his calling in the gap between what technology could do and what most businesses actually used it for.

The move to Dubai was not seamless. He started from zero in a city that waits for no one · freelancing, rebuilding, even working in electronics retail while he built his foundation. That period taught him resilience, humility, and the reality of building in a new market. When the opportunity came in the Zoho ecosystem, he recognized the door he had been waiting for · and walked through it.

Today, Murtaza leads multiple ventures across digital transformation, blockchain fintech, financial markets, and a full-service technology group. His founding philosophy is simple: integrity above everything. Real value. Transparent pricing. Work that speaks for itself.

The Challenge
  • Tank inspection required full 3–5 day production shutdown per cycle costing millions in lost output
  • Human entry into confined spaces and explosive atmospheres creating unacceptable safety risk
  • Inspections conducted only quarterly — anomalies often detected too late
  • Manual paper-based data — no digital trail, no predictive maintenance capability
